# Portfolio Home / Paper Cinema

## Scope and mode

- Experience surface for engineering leaders, hiring managers, senior engineers, and technical collaborators.
- Job: make Hatim's technical range memorable, then expose evidence-backed ownership, chronological work, and direct contact routes.
- Primary action: move from the opening title into the newest-first project programme.

## Chosen direction

- World: **Paper Cinema / Kinetic Cut-and-Paste**—an experimental title sequence and engineering production notebook directed frame by frame.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/paper-cinema/split-screen-jump-cut.png`.
- Approval: delegated by the user's explicit unique design prompt and full creative choice for this isolated variant.
- Memorable moment: cream title strips on projector black collide with a cadmium-yellow paper-flight stage and a vertical verified-evidence reel.
- Do not literalize: invented client fields, buildings, fake project screenshots, unverified impact language, or any placeholder data in generated comps.

## Component grammar and constraints

- Full-palette strategy: projector black and cream are the base stages; yellow, cyan, magenta, and vermilion own whole scenes.
- Type: Barlow Condensed for opening titles and one-sheet names; Clash Display for narrative; monospace only for timecode/data.
- Corners remain square; torn silhouettes come from clipped paper planes, not generic rounded containers.
- Motion is concentrated in the hero flight and contact-sheet exposures; `prefers-reduced-motion` renders a static complete frame.
- Desktop uses a three-stage hero; mobile becomes sequential black title, yellow flight, and 2×2 evidence reel with no lost content.
- Private projects have no dead repository links. All touch actions remain at least 44px high and all links keep visible focus.
