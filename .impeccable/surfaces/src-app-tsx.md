# Portfolio Home — Signal Press

## Scope and visitor

- Experience-mode portfolio for engineering leaders, hiring managers, senior engineers, and collaborators.
- The visitor should recognize systems breadth quickly, inspect evidence, explore newest-first work, and contact Hatim.

## Direction and memorable moment

- World: Signal Press / Kinetic Type Foundry, a radical independent engineering magazine printed live at monumental scale.
- A two-plate HATIM SHAKIR cover locks into register like metal sorts while verified contribution figures occupy the proof edge.
- UnifyApps becomes an investigative centerfold; projects become numbered collectible feature spreads; contribution history becomes an overprinted statistical poster.

## Content and action

- Preserve authenticated GitHub metrics and methodology, all UnifyApps ownership evidence, Salesforce, project chronology and access states, résumé, social, repository, live, and email links.
- Primary action: enter the newest-first work; closing action: email Hatim or inspect the résumé/profile links.

## Constraints

- React/Vite single page, semantic landmarks, keyboard focus, 44px touch targets, 390px re-composition, reduced-motion alternative, and no horizontal overflow.
- No detector/collider language, conventional dashboard, generic card grid, glass, fake glossy finish, or concept mock used as a production background.
- Unresolved decisions: none; the user pinned the direction and granted full creative authority.
