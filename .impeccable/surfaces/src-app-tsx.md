# Portfolio Home — Orbital Command Deck

## Scope and visitor mode

- Target: `src/App.tsx`, with `index.html`, `src/App.css`, `src/index.css`, and `src/components/MissionScope.tsx` as the implementation boundary.
- Mode: Experience. Engineering leaders, hiring managers, senior engineers, and technical collaborators should identify Hatim's systems ownership in one viewport, inspect evidence, then open work or make contact.

## Direction and approval

- World: an elite 1987 analog orbital mission-control deck upgraded for AI systems; physical avionics, not a terminal theme or glossy sci-fi HUD.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/command-deck-c.png`, selected under the user's delegated creative authority because its three-bay topology makes identity, live evidence, and control status simultaneously legible.
- Memorable moment: a real canvas oscilloscope lives between Hatim's mission-lead bay and authenticated mechanical counters; Signal, Route, and Live controls alter the deck state.
- Adaptation: the comp's paper flight plan is omitted from the first viewport so the live, reduced-motion-aware system trace remains the focal proof. Paper returns as the authenticated contribution recorder.
