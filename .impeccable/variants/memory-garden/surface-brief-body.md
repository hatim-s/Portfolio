## Component grammar

- Forms are asymmetric botanical husks, rings, membranes, and connective fibers; repeated content never becomes a card grid.
- Lines are 1–2px moon/cyan/chlorophyll roots. Ember marks rare hot nodes, never general decoration.
- Depth comes from overlapping translucent membranes and bounded glows; there is no glass panel system.
- Gloock carries living display forms; Clash Display carries all interface and narrative text; no technical-costume monospace.
- Motion is concentrated in the hero organism and radial branch bloom. Offscreen canvas work pauses; reduced motion renders a composed still.

- Desktop uses a two-part clearing/trunk composition; at 390px the organism becomes a full-bleed backdrop, metrics reflow to two columns, and every later section becomes a single rooted stream.
- Touch targets remain at least 44px; no function relies on hover.
- Private specimens expose no dead source link; all external links retain semantic names.
