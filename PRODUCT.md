## Product Principles

1. Lead with the artifact: the first viewport must make Hatim's engineering world tangible before it explains it.
2. Proof over adjectives: every claim connects to a project, contribution pattern, architecture, or shipped interaction.
3. Range with a spine: systems, agents, product UI, and developer tools should feel like one practice, not unrelated badges.
4. Motion as navigation: cinematic movement should reveal structure and causality, never obscure reading or access.
5. Private stays private: describe the product truth while protecting source and inaccessible links.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings and landmarks, 44px touch targets, and a complete `prefers-reduced-motion` experience.
